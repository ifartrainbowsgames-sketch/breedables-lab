**Beginner**

**[Adobe Creative Cloud — 3D texturing](https://www.youtube.com/@AdobeCreativeCloud)** — Official Photoshop 3D/paint workflows

**[Get Started with Photoshop](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/tutorials.html)** — Adobe's official tutorial hub covers core tools and layers needed to start painting creature textures.

**[Selections and Masking in Photoshop](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/masking-layers.html)** — Strong masking fundamentals let you isolate fur, skin, and scale zones while texturing.

**Intermediate**

**[Photoshop — PBR texture cleanup](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/3d-painting.html)** — Industry 2D finish on exported maps

**[Photo Compositing in Photoshop](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/compositing.html)** — Compositing workflows help combine renders and paint-over passes for final creature lookdev.

**[Retouching and Repairing Images](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/retouching-repairing.html)** — Retouching tools are essential for cleaning up baked texture maps and removing artifacts.

**[Painting and Drawing in Photoshop](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/painting-drawing.html)** — Brush engines and blending modes are the heart of hand-painted creature skin and fur details.

**Advanced**

**[Working with Filters and Effects](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/filters.html)** — Filters add stylized surface effects and can quickly iterate mood and material direction.

# Tools

!!! abstract "This section answers one question"
    *Which tool do I use, and how do I learn it?*

Every tool the studio uses, evaluates or deliberately avoids has exactly one
page here. Whatever you need to know about a tool — what it costs, whether we
are allowed to ship with it, and the lessons that teach it — is on that tool's
page, not scattered across the pipeline.

The [production pipeline](../pipeline.md) tells you *which stage* you are in.
This section tells you *what to open*.

!!! info "We link to the vendor's manual, we do not copy it"
    The Blender Manual already explains and pictures every button, and it is
    maintained by people who work on Blender. Re-typing it here would only
    create a worse copy that goes stale. Our pages carry the part no vendor
    manual can: the Second Life constraints, the polygon budgets, the export
    settings and the evidence each lesson has to produce.

## The one we teach

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ![Blender](../assets/inline/blender.png){ width="96" }

    ### [Blender](blender/index.md)

    **Free · GPL v3 · USE NOW**

    The studio default. It covers modelling, sculpting, retopology, UV, baking,
    texturing, rigging, animation and Second Life export in one package, which
    is why it is the only tool taught end to end.

## Modelling & sculpting

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ![Autodesk Maya](../assets/inline/maya.png){ width="72" }

    ### [Autodesk Maya](maya/index.md)

    Paid · subscription

    The industry modelling and rigging standard. Learn the vocabulary; we do not
    require it.

-   ![ZBrush](../assets/inline/zbrush.png){ width="72" }

    ### [ZBrush](zbrush/index.md)

    Paid · Maxon

    The industry sculpting standard. Blender sculpt covers our needs today.

-   ![TopoGun](../assets/inline/topogun.png){ width="72" }

    ### [TopoGun](topogun/index.md)

    Paid · standalone

    Dedicated retopology and baking. Rarely needed alongside Blender.

-   ### [RetopoFlow](retopoflow/index.md)

    Free · GPL — **licence under review**

    Blender retopology add-on. The code is GPL; the bundled assets still need an
    audit before we ship anything made with it.

-   ![PureRef](../assets/inline/pureref.png){ width="72" }

    ### [PureRef](pureref/index.md)

    Free tier · paid for business

    Reference boards. The free tier is enough for personal study.

</div>

## Texturing & look development

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ![Substance 3D Painter](../assets/inline/substance-painter.png){ width="72" }

    ### [Substance 3D Painter](substance-painter/index.md)

    Paid · Adobe subscription

    The industry texturing standard. Compare against our free stack before

    Free · MIT

    Procedural PBR generator. Part of the studio texturing route.

    Free · open source

    Layer-based painting inside Blender. Part of the studio texturing route.

    ### [Adobe Photoshop](photoshop/index.md)

## Before you adopt anything new

Nothing becomes a studio tool because it looks good in a video. A candidate has
to carry a licence audit and evidence before it appears above.

|------|-------|

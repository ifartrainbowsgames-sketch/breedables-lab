# Blender foundations path

!!! info "About this path"
    **For** — anyone who has never opened Blender  
    **Ends with** — a rigged, textured creature that uploads and animates correctly in Second Life  
    **Length** — ten lessons, each producing committed evidence in `training/`

This path is an **order to read things in**. Every lesson lives on its subject
page, which is also where the software, official documentation, videos and
comparisons for that subject live. Do the ten in sequence the first time;
afterwards use the subject pages directly as reference.

## The sequence

| # | Lesson | You will be able to… | Then prove it |
|---|--------|----------------------|---------------|
| 1 | [Install & setup](../../tools/blender/install-and-setup.md) | Install the right LTS and configure units/viewport for SL scale | — |
| 2 | [Interface & navigation](../../tools/blender/interface-and-navigation.md) | Move, select and transform confidently | — |
| 3 | [Mesh modeling](../../tools/blender/mesh-modeling.md) | Box-model a clean organic creature base | — |

## What "done" looks like

After lesson 10 you can take an idea for a breedable creature and produce:

1. A sculpted, retopologised mesh with a sensible poly budget.
2. Clean UVs and a metallic/roughness material that survives upload.
3. A quadruped rig with idle and walk animations.
4. An SL upload package — mesh, LODs, physics, material — with documented land impact.

## Before you start

- A machine meeting Blender's [minimum requirements](https://www.blender.org/download/requirements/).
- A Second Life account and viewer for the in-world checks in lessons 7 and 10.
- Read the [Academy overview](../index.md) once, so you know why every lesson demands an artifact.

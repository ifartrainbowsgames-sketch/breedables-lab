## Role

Primary modeling, sculpting, UV, baking, rigging, and animation environment for the studio.

## Breedables use

- Organic creature meshes and variants
- PBR material authoring and export
- Rigify-based quadruped rigs (see [experiments](../experiments.md) E04)
- glTF / Second Life upload preparation

## Notes

Bundled add-ons such as [Rigify](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/rigify/index.html) are part of the baseline rigging curriculum.
